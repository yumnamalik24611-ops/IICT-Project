Event Organization Rules

  1. Submit event proposals before the planned event date.
  2. Obtain approval from the relevant university department.
  3. Respect university facilities, staff and fellow students.
  4. Maintain safety and discipline throughout the activity.
  5. Leave the venue clean and return borrowed equipment.
